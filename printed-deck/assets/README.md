# Location outlines

`locations.json` contains simplified mainland country outlines for South Korea, France and Austria from Natural Earth 1:110m admin-0 country data. Natural Earth data is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ . Source URL is also recorded in the JSON.

The three country panels are independently scaled location illustrations, not a comparative area or distance map. City markers identify Seoul, Busan, Paris and Vienna.
